## Verified Obstruction From The Old Model

The old alpha \(=1\) source model uses
\[
E,\ H_x,\ H_y,\ P,\ P_{z,1},\ P_{z,2},\ Q_x,\ Q_y .
\]
With
\[
a_x=\frac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{\epsilon_0},\qquad
a_y=\frac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{\epsilon_0},
\]
the shifted magnetic variables
\[
M_x=Q_x-\mu a_xH_x,\qquad M_y=Q_y-\mu a_yH_y
\]
satisfy
\[
M_{x,t}=a_x\partial_yE-\frac{\sigma_y}{\epsilon_0}M_x,\qquad
M_{y,t}=-a_y\partial_xE-\frac{\sigma_x}{\epsilon_0}M_y .
\]

In an \(x\)-edge PML with constant \(\sigma_x=s>0,\sigma_y=0\), the ansatz
\[
E=P=P_{z,1}=P_{z,2}=H_y=Q_y=0
\]
with spatially constant \(H_x,Q_x\) gives
\[
M_x=Q_x+\mu\frac{s}{\epsilon_0}H_x=\text{constant}
\]
and therefore
\[
H_x(t)=H_x(0)+\frac{M_x(0)}{\mu}t,\qquad
Q_x(t)=Q_x(0)-\frac{s}{\epsilon_0}M_x(0)t .
\]
The obstruction is the undamped shifted magnetic auxiliary in the edge
direction.  Since this exact solution has \(P=0\), changing only the Debye or
fractional polarization law cannot remove it.

## Repair 1: Compatibility Restriction

For the old equations, the minimal compatibility condition that kills the
verified constant edge mode is
\[
Q_x(0)+\mu\frac{\sigma_x}{\epsilon_0}H_x(0)=0
\quad\text{on an \(x\)-edge zero-transverse mode,}
\]
and symmetrically
\[
Q_y(0)+\mu\frac{\sigma_y}{\epsilon_0}H_y(0)=0
\quad\text{on a \(y\)-edge zero-transverse mode.}
\]
This is physically meaningful if the \(Q_i\) are not independent data but
history variables generated by a PML construction.

Candidate energy:
\[
\mathcal E_0=\frac12\int_\Omega
\left(\epsilon_0\epsilon_\infty E^2+\mu H_x^2+\mu H_y^2
+\chi^{-1}P^2+P_{z,1}^2+P_{z,2}^2+Q_x^2+Q_y^2\right)\,dxdy .
\]
It is positive, and the previous verified result gives
\[
\mathcal E_0'(t)\le C_G\mathcal E_0(t).
\]

Classification: useful but partial.  In a genuine 2D \(x\)-edge,
\[
M_{x,t}=-\frac{\sigma_x}{\epsilon_0}\partial_yE,
\]
so pointwise \(M_x=0\) is not invariant for arbitrary two-dimensional data.
This route removes the verified zero-transverse free mode but does not produce
a full 2D monotone energy for the old model.

## Repair 2: Shifted-Variable MPML Damping

Keep the old shifted variables but add edge-positive damping
\(\eta_x,\eta_y\ge0\):
\[
Q_{x,t}=-(b_x+\eta_x)Q_x+\mu(b_x+\eta_x)a_xH_x,
\]
\[
Q_{y,t}=-(b_y+\eta_y)Q_y+\mu(b_y+\eta_y)a_yH_y,
\]
where \(b_i=\sigma_i/\epsilon_0\).  Equivalently,
\[
M_{x,t}=a_x\partial_yE-(b_y+\eta_x)M_x,\qquad
M_{y,t}=-a_y\partial_xE-(b_x+\eta_y)M_y .
\]

For the old \(x\)-edge free subsystem this becomes
\[
H_{x,t}=\frac{M_x}{\mu},\qquad M_{x,t}=-\eta_xM_x .
\]
If \(\eta_x>0\), then
\[
\mathcal E_{x,\eta}
=\frac{\mu}{2}\left|H_x+\frac{M_x}{\mu\eta_x}\right|^2
+\frac{c_x}{2}|M_x|^2,\qquad c_x>0,
\]
is positive and satisfies
\[
\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal E_{x,\eta}=-c_x\eta_x|M_x|^2\le0 .
\]

Classification: monotone for the isolated edge free mode and a plausible
MPML/CFS repair of the old Jordan block.  In full 2D it still leaves
\(M_x\partial_yE\) and \(M_y\partial_xE\) couplings, so the completed theorem
would need derivative, nonlocal, or Baffet-type structure.

## Selected Model: Covariantly Damped Maxwell-Debye Layer

The strongest verified constructive result is a modified alpha \(=1\)
Maxwell-Debye absorbing-layer model.  Let
\[
\rho_x,\rho_y\in L^\infty(\Omega),\qquad \rho_x,\rho_y\ge0,\qquad
\rho=\rho_x+\rho_y,\qquad D_\rho=\partial_t+\rho .
\]
The profiles vanish in the physical region and are positive in the absorbing
layer.

The model is
\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\epsilon_0\epsilon_\infty D_\rho E
&=\partial_xH_y-\partial_yH_x-D_\rho P,\\
\mu(\partial_t+\rho_y)H_x&=-\partial_yE,\\
\mu(\partial_t+\rho_x)H_y&=\partial_xE,\\
P+\tau D_\rho P&=\chi E,
\end{aligned}}
\]
with PEC boundary condition \(E=0\) on \(\partial\Omega\).

This model removes the independent \(P_{z,i},Q_i\) variables.  The old edge
auxiliary mode is therefore absent.  The important design choice is applying
the same covariant derivative \(D_\rho\) to the Debye law; this changes the old
sign-indefinite \(EP\) damping term into positive polarization dissipation.

## Energy Functional

Use
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_\rho(t)=\frac12\int_\Omega
\left(\epsilon_0\epsilon_\infty E^2+\mu H_x^2+\mu H_y^2+\chi^{-1}P^2\right)
\,dxdy .}
\]
This energy is positive definite because
\(\epsilon_0,\epsilon_\infty,\mu,\chi>0\).

## Energy Identity

Multiplying the equations by \(E,H_x,H_y\), integrating, and using PEC gives
the usual Maxwell cancellation:
\[
\int_\Omega(E\partial_xH_y-E\partial_yH_x-H_x\partial_yE+H_y\partial_xE)\,dxdy=0 .
\]
The covariant Debye law gives
\[
E\,D_\rho P
=\frac{1}{2\chi}\partial_t(P^2)
+\frac{\rho}{\chi}P^2
+\frac{\tau}{\chi}|D_\rho P|^2 .
\]
Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\frac{d}{dt}\mathcal E_\rho(t)
=-\int_\Omega\left(
\epsilon_0\epsilon_\infty\rho E^2
+\mu\rho_yH_x^2
+\mu\rho_xH_y^2
+\frac{\rho}{\chi}P^2
+\frac{\tau}{\chi}|D_\rho P|^2
\right)\,dxdy\le0 .}
\]
Thus
\[
\boxed{\mathcal E_\rho(t)\le \mathcal E_\rho(0)\quad\text{for all }t\ge0.}
\]

Classification: monotone decreasing, dissipative, and uniformly bounded in
long time.  It is a repaired absorbing-layer model, not a proof that the old
eight-variable unsplit source PML is monotone.

## Remaining Open Problems

1. Exact perfect matching for the selected covariantly damped model is not
proved here.  The verified result is stability and layer dissipation.
2. The shifted MPML repair needs a full 2D derivative or nonlocal energy to
control \(M_x\partial_yE\) and \(M_y\partial_xE\).
3. The compatibility route should be sharpened by Fourier analysis of the
transverse zero-mode projection and by a boundary-compatible definition of the
allowed old-model data.
4. The fractional case \(0<\alpha<1\) can be added later by replacing the
alpha \(=1\) Debye identity with a positive fractional-memory representation;
it cannot remove the old edge obstruction by itself because that obstruction
has \(P=0\).
