## statement

For constant nonnegative PML coefficients sigma_x,sigma_y with a=sigma_x+sigma_y>0 and positive Debye parameters, the Baffet-type primitive formulation in artifacts/proofs/stage3_baffet_debye_2d_energy.md is Laplace-equivalent to the standard stretched-coordinate Maxwell-Debye PML, hence retains planar-interface perfect matching, and has the displayed positive energy E_BD satisfying dE_BD/dt=-D_BD plus boundary flux, with D_BD nonnegative. Under PEC or canceling transmission flux, E_BD is non-increasing and gives a no-abnormal-growth estimate with the nullspace/gauge limitations stated in the artifact.
