## statement

If a physical subregion has zero PML damping, then E=0, P=0, all PML auxiliary variables zero, and H=(H_x,H_y)=grad phi with phi smooth and compactly supported in that undamped physical subregion gives an exact stationary Maxwell-Debye solution whenever curl H=partial_x H_y-partial_y H_x=0. The magnetic energy is positive for grad phi nonzero while all layer and Debye damping terms vanish. Hence no positive total energy controlling the magnetic field can be strictly decreasing for every nonzero state on the whole computational domain; any dissipative identity must have a characterized nullspace containing such undamped stationary modes.
