## statement

For Maxwell-Debye with A(s)=epsilon0 epsilon_infty+chi/(1+tau s), the standard stretched-coordinate PML keeps A(s) and replaces partial_i by S_i(s)^{-1} partial_i, S_i=1+sigma_i/s.  At a planar x-interface with S_x=(s+rho)/s, S_y=1, the transmitted normal number is kappa_sc=S_x kappa_0 and the admittance is Y_sc=-kappa_sc/(mu s S_x)=-kappa_0/(mu s), hence the reflection coefficient is zero for every outgoing physical mode.  In the one-dimensional x-PML normal-incidence block, the exact stretched system (partial_t+rho)(epsilon0 epsilon_infty E+P)=partial_x H_y, mu(partial_t+rho)H_y=partial_xE, P+tau P_t=chi E has the positive energy integral 1/2 int (epsilon0 epsilon_infty E^2+mu H_y^2+(1+rho tau)chi^{-1}P^2) dx and derivative -int (rho epsilon0 epsilon_infty E^2+mu rho H_y^2+rho chi^{-1}P^2+tau chi^{-1}P_t^2) dx, up to the usual boundary flux.
