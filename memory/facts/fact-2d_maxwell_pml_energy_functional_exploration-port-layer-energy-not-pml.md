## statement

For the port model epsilon E_t + P_t + epsilon sigma_E E = partial_x W_y - partial_y W_x, mu W_{x,t}+mu sigma_y W_x=-partial_y E, mu W_{y,t}+mu sigma_x W_y=partial_x E, tau P_t+P=kappa E, the positive energy 1/2 integral (epsilon E^2+mu W_x^2+mu W_y^2+kappa^{-1}P^2) satisfies dE_port/dt plus integral(epsilon sigma_E E^2+mu sigma_y W_x^2+mu sigma_x W_y^2+tau kappa^{-1}P_t^2) equals the boundary flux integral E(W_y n_x-W_x n_y). However, in an x-PML with sigma_x=sigma>0 and sigma_y=0, the Laplace-Fourier admittance obeys Y_port^2-Y_0^2=(epsilon sigma_E-sigma epsilon_D(s))/(mu(s+sigma))-sigma k_y^2/(mu^2 s^2(s+sigma)). Therefore no scalar local sigma_E makes Y_port=Y_0 for all oblique modes k_y, so the port model is a stable absorbing layer, not a strict perfectly matched layer, although it reduces to Maxwell-Debye when all damping vanishes.
