## statement

For the repaired Maxwell-Debye PML with Laplace stretch factors S_i(s) applied to spatial derivatives and with the physical Debye law left as (1+tau s)P=chi E, a planar x-interface with S_y=1 has transmitted normal symbol nu=S_x nu0 and admittance Y_+=S_x^{-1}nu/(mu s)=nu0/(mu s)=Y0, so the continuous interface is perfectly matched. In a time-domain ADE realization of the classical stretch S_i=1+sigma_i/s, the positive physical Debye energy E_phys=1/2 int(eps0 eps_inf E^2+mu H_x^2+mu H_y^2+chi^{-1}P^2) has derivative -int tau/chi P_t^2 plus the sign-indefinite memory couplings int[E(psi_E,x-psi_E,y)-H_x psi_Hx,y+H_y psi_Hy,x]. Therefore the v2 monotone energy identity does not transfer to the exact stretched-coordinate PML without a new augmented/passive-memory energy. See artifacts/proofs/stage3_repaired_model_energy.md.
